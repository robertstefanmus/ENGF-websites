DEPARTMENT FOR DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION & INNOVATION IN EDUCATION

ENGF Digital
Education Initiative

The Initiative works county by county with school leaders, local authorities and experts: informing them about available education funding, researching schools' digital needs, building digital skills and the responsible use of AI, and connecting institutions with the people who can help.

CAMPAIGNS RESEARCH RESPONSIBLE AI
PROGRAMME OF WORK
01

Information Campaigns

County-by-county sessions and briefings for mayors, school leaders and administrators.

02

Institutional Cooperation

Work alongside county councils, school inspectorates and regional development agencies.

03

Funding Information

Accessible guidance on education funding available to schools and authorities.

04

Digital Skills & Responsible AI

Training and guidance on the considered use of digital tools and artificial intelligence in teaching.

05

Needs Research & Impact

Assessment of schools' digital requirements and of the results of intervention.

The Initiative provides information, education and facilitation. It is not a funding authority, a managing authority, or an intermediary in the award of public funds.

What partnership involves

Co-organising conferences, roundtables and academic sessions.
Hosting fellows, study visits or institutional dialogue.
Commissioning or contributing to research and policy work.
Supporting courses, open calls, exhibitions or community programmes.

Partnerships are established in writing, with defined objectives and published attribution. ENGF remains independent and politically neutral in all cooperation.

INTRODUCTION

ENGF exists to give young people a permanent, serious route into the questions Europe is deciding. We organise education and professional development, conduct research and publish analysis, and convene dialogue between the next generation and those who shape policy — in cooperation with universities, public institutions, civil society, international organisations and the private sector.

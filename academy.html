DEPARTMENT FOR EDUCATION & PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

ENGF Academy

The Academy delivers non-formal education for students and early-career professionals: structured courses, workshops, masterclasses and summer schools taught by practitioners, with applied projects, simulations and study visits.

Mentors & Trainers

Sessions led by academics, civil servants, diplomats and professionals in the relevant field.

ENGF Academy programmes are non-formal educational activities. No programme is described as accredited unless and until accreditation is formally confirmed.
